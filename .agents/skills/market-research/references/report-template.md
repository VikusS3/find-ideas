# Report Template

Follow this structure. Keep the full report roughly 800–2000 words depending on evidence found — dense and evidence-linked, not padded. Every material claim gets a source link inline like `([source](url))`.

---

---

## Style rules

- Verdict section comes early (section 2) — busy readers stop there.
- Quotes from communities: keep under 15 words each, always linked.
- Numbers without sources don't go in the report. Estimates are labeled as estimates.
- Tables for competitors and the scorecard; prose elsewhere. No filler bullets.
- File name: `market-research-<idea-slug>.md` (lowercase, hyphenated).
